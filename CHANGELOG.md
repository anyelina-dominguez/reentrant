# Changelog

## 0.4.0

The day end to end, and the paperwork round it.

- the download tent: a card in, a slip and a result out
- corrections, complaints, the jury, and backup punching
- hire cards, start blocks, late starts
- club team scoring, a winter league, and course statistics
- the board outside the tent, which says it is provisional
- the organiser's report, the season's fixtures
- an http surface over all of it, and fourteen command line commands

## 0.3.0

- entries, age classes worked from the age on 31 December, and the entry importer
- the start draw, keeping clubmates and families apart
- punches, whether a run counts, splits and split analysis
- the results list, relays and age handicaps
- the event file, saved atomically, with migrations

## 0.2.0

- controls, IOF control descriptions, and the separation rules
- courses, legs, length and climb
- the checks a controller makes: dog-legs, short legs, bunching
- reading and writing the course and control files that pass between laptops

## 0.1.0

- errors, argument checking, pinned sorting
- race time as whole seconds
- British National Grid references at every precision people write them in
